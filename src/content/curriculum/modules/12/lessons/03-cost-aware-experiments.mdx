<ConceptSection layout="split">
  <div className="learning-concept-copy">
Before a lab, list its resources, region, size, expected runtime and cleanup procedure. Check the current calculator,
offer conditions and account billing status. A guest shutdown can leave an Azure VM stopped and allocated, with
compute billing continuing. Check for the deallocated power state to confirm that VM compute billing stopped.
Inventory disks, addresses and other resources separately because their charges can continue. See
[Azure VM states and billing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/virtual-machines/states-billing).

A budget alert helps you notice spend. Verify account limits before provisioning, and choose the local lab path
when you only need to practice the resource relationships and cleanup reasoning.
