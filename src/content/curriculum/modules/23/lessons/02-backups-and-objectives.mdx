<ConceptSection layout="split">
  <div className="learning-concept-copy">
    <h4>A backup is a recovery input</h4>
    <p>A completed backup job shows that a process ran. It does not by itself show that the artifact is complete, readable, consistent, available to the recovery team, or usable by the application. A restore drill tests those assumptions against a separate target.</p>
    <p><strong>Recovery Point Objective (RPO)</strong> is the maximum data-loss interval the plan is designed to tolerate. <strong>Recovery Time Objective (RTO)</strong> is the target duration for restoring the required service. Both are requirements to compare with measured results; neither is a performance claim until the relevant recovery path has been tested.</p>
    <p>Protect backup copies from the same failure and credentials that can affect the source. A checksum can detect byte changes, while a database integrity check and an application read test answer different questions.</p>
  </div>
