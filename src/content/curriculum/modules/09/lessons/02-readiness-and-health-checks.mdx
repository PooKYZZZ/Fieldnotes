<ConceptSection layout="split">
  <div className="learning-concept-copy">
Starting containers in order does not make a dependency ready. A database may have a running process but still be
recovering, applying initialization or refusing connections. A health check can report a meaningful local condition;
the application should also retry transient connections with a bounded delay and a clear error path. Avoid a fixed
sleep that is too short on a slow host and wasteful on a fast one.

Compose can wait for a dependency health condition when configured to do so, but that does not replace runtime
retry logic: dependencies can fail after startup. Choose a health test that checks the capability the next service
actually needs, not just that a PID exists.
