    <p>If 98 of 100 eligible requests succeed, the observed ratio for that sample is 98%. For a request-based objective of 99% over the same sample, the allowance is one unsuccessful request; two failures exceed it by one. This arithmetic only applies if the numerator, denominator, eligibility rules and time window match the stated objective. A tiny sample is highly variable, so do not claim production reliability from it. State the data source, window, exclusions and limitations alongside the result.</p> <p>Use an error-budget policy as a decision aid, not a universal rule. For example, a team might pause a risky rollout after a sustained increase in measured failures, inspect the deployment and user-impact evidence, then decide whether to continue. A single small sample should prompt investigation rather than an automatic policy response. Match the action to the system's impact and measurement quality.</p>
