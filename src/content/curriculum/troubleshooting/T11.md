# Rollout stuck

## Observation

A Kubernetes Deployment never reaches desired availability.

## Investigation

Inspect Pods, events, readiness, image fetch, resource requests and rollout history.


## Safety constraint

Work only on disposable resources or services you own, and record initial evidence before applying a
fix.

## Hint

A Pod in Running state may still be NotReady.

## Completion evidence

Provide a cause hypothesis, evidence that differentiates it from another possible cause, a minimal fix
and a repeatable verification step.
