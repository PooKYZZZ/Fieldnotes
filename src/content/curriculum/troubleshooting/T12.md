# Unverified backup

## Observation

A backup job reports success but the restore procedure fails.

## Investigation

Use an isolated restore target; inspect archive format, schema version and missing prerequisites.

## Safety constraint

Work only on disposable resources or services you own, and record initial evidence before applying a
fix.

## Hint

A backup is validated by restoration, not by its creation log.

## Completion evidence

Provide a cause hypothesis, evidence that differentiates it from another possible cause, a minimal fix
and a repeatable verification step.
Spaced review and recall prompts
At a later point, randomly pick one finished activity and reproduce it in a fresh lab. Use documentation but do not
open your own solution until you have made a first attempt. The educational rationale comes from retrieval/spacing
research, which supports thoughtful recall practice but does not mandate a particular study calendar.
For each practice module, ask: (1) What assumption did I get wrong? (2) What evidence changed my mind? (3) Can I
explain this in an interview? (4) What would the safer production behavior be?
