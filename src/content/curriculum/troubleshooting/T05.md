# CI works locally

## Observation

A test passes on your laptop and fails on a clean GitHub runner.

## Investigation

Compare dependencies, path assumptions, environment variables, platform and test isolation.

## Safety constraint

Work only on disposable resources or services you own, and record initial evidence before applying a
fix.

## Hint

A hidden local dependency is evidence, not proof that the runner is wrong.

## Completion evidence

Provide a cause hypothesis, evidence that differentiates it from another possible cause, a minimal fix
and a repeatable verification step.
