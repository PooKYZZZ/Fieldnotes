# Permission denied

## Observation

A service runs interactively under your account but not under its systemd account.

## Investigation

Inspect unit User, working directory, file and directory modes, and journal messages.

## Safety constraint

Work only on disposable resources or services you own, and record initial evidence before applying a
fix.

## Hint

Do not solve access problems by making everything world-writable.

## Completion evidence

Provide a cause hypothesis, evidence that differentiates it from another possible cause, a minimal fix
and a repeatable verification step.
