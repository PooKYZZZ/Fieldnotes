# Cloud access denied

## Observation

SSH times out, but the Azure VM reports running.

## Investigation

1. Confirm the VM and network are yours to investigate. Record the exact target and time; verify that the address is current without copying it into a public record.
2. On the authorized client, check the selected route and whether the connection times out or is refused. A timeout alone does not identify an NSG, route or guest-firewall cause.
3. In the provider's read-only control-plane view, verify VM power state, attached network interface, effective route and the narrowly scoped inbound rule for the intended source and SSH port.
4. Through the VM console or another authorized management path, check the guest route, firewall and listener with `ip route` and `ss -ltn`. Do not disable the firewall or open SSH to the internet as a test.
5. Compare guest account/key authorization only after network reachability is established. Test one reversible rule change in an isolated lab and confirm the original access path is restored.

## Safety constraint

Work only on disposable resources or services you own, and record initial evidence before applying a
fix.

## Hint

Control-plane login permission and guest SSH login are different.

## Completion evidence

Differentiate the address/route, provider rule, guest firewall, listener and guest-login checks. Show the authorized source-to-port path before and after the narrow lab change, and confirm no public exposure was added.
