# Terraform surprises

## Observation

Terraform proposes replacing a resource after you changed a seemingly minor variable.

## Investigation

Inspect plan and provider attributes; determine whether destruction would lose data.

## Safety constraint

Work only on disposable resources or services you own, and record initial evidence before applying a
fix.

## Hint

Do not apply or refresh state carelessly merely to remove a warning.

## Completion evidence

Provide a cause hypothesis, evidence that differentiates it from another possible cause, a minimal fix
and a repeatable verification step.
